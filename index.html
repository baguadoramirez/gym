SARS-CoV-2 (April, 2020)

Due to the ongoing Coronavirus (SARS-CoV-2) crisis, I’ve developed a Shiny library to track the progression of the data. The app is available in Spanish and is designed to be as user-friendly as possible, allowing anyone to easily interpret the data without needing any prior knowledge.

If you’re interested, you can check out the application at the following link (Click here!). It’s a simple but effective tool that has proven to be incredibly helpful during these challenging times to friends and relatives. I hope it provides you with valuable insights into the evolution of the data, and feel free to share it with anyone who might find it useful.

Please, stay safe!
